How to Play

Controls:
- Move: W, A, S, D
- Jump: Space (Double-tap Space for jumping in the building roof, go near the building)
- Shoot: Left Click
- Sniper Mode: Right Click (hold then release to shoot)

Objective:
- Defeat enemies and collect 5 gold coins.
- After defeating all normal enemies, a boss will appear.
- Defeat the boss to spawn a portal.
- Step through the portal with 5 gold coins to win the game.

Tools:
- Life lines ❤️
- Gold coins 🪙
- Three guns scattered in the ground
