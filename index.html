Game One

Clear the map, grab the gold, and escape through the portal.

🎯 Objective

  1. Defeat the 6 enemies roaming the map.
  2. Collect 5 gold coins 🪙 scattered around.
  3. When the last enemy falls, a boss appears.
  4. Defeat the boss to open a portal.
  5. Walk into the portal with 5 coins to win.

🕹️ Controls

  • W A S D Move
  • Space Jump
  • Space Space Double-tap near a building to jump to its roof
  • Left Click Shoot
  • Right Click Hold to aim, release to snipe
  • V Toggle third-person view / in-car cockpit view
  • N Toggle night mode
  • Esc Pause / Resume

🧰 Pickups

  • ❤️ Lifeline — restores 2 life
  • 🪙 Gold coin — collect 5 to win
  • 🔫 Rifle (blue) — 2 damage
  • 🔫 Shotgun (green) — 5 damage
🔫 Pistol DMG 1
