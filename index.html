Controls

WASD Move
Shift Run
Space Jump · SpaceSpace next to a building: leap onto its roof
F Switch between web shooter and gun
Web shooter: Left click shoot a web at a building ahead · hold to swing · release to fly · click again mid-air to chain · WASD pump the swing
Gun: Left click Shoot · Right click hold to aim
E Get in / out of cars and bikes (works on moving traffic too)
Driving: WS gas / reverse · AD steer · Space brake · Shift boost
M Full map (scroll to zoom, drag to pan)
N Skip ahead to night / morning (time passes on its own: a full day ≈ 12 min)
H Horn (while driving)   K Mute / unmute sound
Mouse Look (click to lock pointer)
Wheel Camera distance
Esc Release pointer
Buildings with a lit sign (amber on the minimap) can be entered —
walk in, take the stairs up, explore the rooms and the roof.
